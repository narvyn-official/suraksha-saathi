# Ordered procedure training — pilot 0.5

Fire and gas/confined-space procedure screens are available from their lesson pages, alongside the existing lessons, component recognition and scored decision assessments. Each supports guided and independent attempts, optional ARCore placement, camera-off scene controls and text actions. They are draft generic training sequences, not site instructions or practical qualifications.

Fire has 12 actions: alarm, authorisation/escape checks, equipment label, pin, aim, squeeze, three sweep positions, withdrawal as conditions worsen, clear exit and assembly. Cached geometry reflects removed pin, lever/nozzle state, symbolic discharge, persistent fire, clear/blocked exits and assembly marker. Gas has nine actions covering exclusion boundary, permit, isolation, atmosphere verification, hazard-specific PPE, outside attendant, communications, unconfirmed rescue and refusal of entry. No reading comes from an actual gas sensor, and the scenario always ends outside.

Each accepted action is saved transactionally in a separate worker-scoped SQLite procedure journal. Strict replay checks versions, event order, outcomes and derived flags; it is consistency validation, not cryptographic device attestation. Guided unsafe actions require review and retry. Independent unsafe actions stop the attempt. Saving happens before the UI advances; lifecycle/scene revisions reject stale callbacks. Text choices use a repeatable attempt-specific ordering. Screen, camera and text events are attributed separately.

Procedure completion is deliberately non-certifiable. Existing scored assessments and exported trainer records are not silently credited from these new journals. The current procedure journals stay on the phone and do not yet appear in trainer imports. Profiles separate local records but have no PIN or verified identity. Most procedure controls select labelled actions. The 0.5.1 increment adds five [spatial target-hold steps](spatial-procedure-practice.md), explicitly attributed and replay-validated. Neither mode measures hand technique, extinguisher force, safe distances or actual evacuation routes. This is a meaningful procedural prototype, not proof that the brief's two complete validated AR simulations are finished.

AR requires a supported ARCore device, installed services and camera permission. Unsupported or unavailable camera paths retain screen/text alternatives and cannot receive camera credit. Positive tracking/placement, thermal/frame performance and field learning transfer still require physical devices and competent reviewers.
