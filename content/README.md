# Pilot curriculum

Version 0.2.0 contains three modules and 24 decisions in English and Hindi. All text, thresholds and illustrations await competent safety and native-language review. These are simulations, not instructions authorising hazardous work. Santali has not been released.

The machinery draft covers guards, reporting jams, stop versus isolation, authorised work, stored energy, verification, personal locks and controlled handover. Its general energy-control principles were checked against [OSHA 1910.147](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.147). This is a technical reference, not a claim that US requirements establish Indian legal compliance. Site-specific procedures and applicable Indian requirements must be approved by a competent reviewer.

`archive/0.1.0.json` preserves the previous content for server regrading. The current JSON is bundled into Android and mirrored in the trainer dashboard. Old records retain their original content version. A change to decisions or marking requires a new content version and matching archive support.
