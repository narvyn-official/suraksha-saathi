# Pilot curriculum

Version 0.4.0 contains five modules and 40 decisions in English and Hindi. All text, thresholds and illustrations await competent safety and native-language review. These are simulations, not instructions authorising hazardous work. Santali has not been released.

The machinery draft covers guards, reporting jams, stop versus isolation, authorised work, stored energy, verification, personal locks and controlled handover. Its general energy-control principles were checked against [OSHA 1910.147](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.147). This is a technical reference, not a claim that US requirements establish Indian legal compliance. Site-specific procedures and applicable Indian requirements must be approved by a competent reviewer.

The PPE and workplace-exposure draft adds four lessons and eight decisions, including seven safety-critical gates. It covers task-specific selection, damaged equipment, failed exposure controls, particulate-filter limitations, respiratory-programme readiness, fit tests versus seal checks, leaving exposure after equipment failure, and protected storage. The module does not prescribe a respirator for a particular mine or process, assess actual exposure, or grant permission to enter a hazardous area.

General principles were checked on 14 September 2026 against these primary sources:

- [OSHA PPE overview](https://www.osha.gov/personal-protective-equipment): suitable protection supplements exposure controls.
- [OSHA PPE general-industry rule explanation](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/federalregister/1994-04-06): appropriate fit and rejection of damaged equipment.
- [OSHA respiratory-protection programme requirements](https://www.osha.gov/training/library/respiratory-protection/major-requirements): assessed selection, medical evaluation, training, fit, seal checks and equipment care.
- [OSHA respiratory-protection training](https://www.osha.gov/respiratory-protection/training): distinction between a fit test and a user seal check, and the need for site-specific instruction.
- [OSHA respirator types](https://www.osha.gov/etools/respiratory-protection/respirator-selection/air-purifying-atmos-supply): particulate filters do not provide gas protection; air-purifying devices do not supply breathing air.
- [OSHA respiratory-protection programme guidelines](https://www.osha.gov/enforcement/directives/cpl-02-02-054): leave the exposure area when protection fails and follow the applicable response procedure.

These sources support general technical principles, not Indian statutory compliance. The cited US rule explanation and programme guidance include historical material; a competent reviewer must approve current Indian and site-specific requirements before deployment. Hindi remains an unreviewed draft, with medical and technical terminology requiring worker comprehension checks.

The emergency and reporting domain is a proposed fifth domain under the user's design freedom: the supplied brief did not specify all five domain names. It is not represented as an official DGMS syllabus. Its four lessons and eight decisions include six safety-critical gates, covering safe-area notification, non-entry by untrained workers, trained response roles, safe assigned evacuation, accountability, missing-person reports, authorised re-entry and accurate handover. It contains no medical treatment, rescue technique or hazardous shutdown instructions.

Emergency sources checked on 15 September 2026:

- [Government of India ERSS](https://112.gov.in/): 112 is the national emergency-assistance channel for police, fire/rescue and health services. The lesson does not promise local availability, location accuracy or response time, and does not imply this training app connects to ERSS.
- [OSHA emergency action plans](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.38): reporting, assigned escape routes, accountability and designated response responsibilities as general planning principles.
- [OSHA emergency action-plan overview](https://www.osha.gov/etools/evacuation-plans-procedures/eap/): site-specific roles, reporting and trained response.
- [OSHA emergency-plan minimum elements](https://www.osha.gov/etools/evacuation-plans-procedures/eap/minimum-requirements): alarms, route assignments and accountability arrangements must reflect the workplace.
- [OSHA emergency-plan checklist](https://www.osha.gov/etools/evacuation-plans-procedures/eap/develop-implement/checklists): untrained rescue creates risk; report missing people and their last known locations.
- [OSHA emergency preparedness guidance](https://www.osha.gov/emergency-preparedness/getting-started): incident-location and condition handover, assembly reporting and authority-directed re-entry.

The US sources provide general safety principles, not evidence of Indian legal compliance. Evacuation versus shelter instructions, alarms, routes, contacts, permitted communication equipment and responder roles must be approved for each mine or workplace. Search results did not provide sufficiently inspectable NDMA guidance for this module; no NDMA review or endorsement is claimed. English and Hindi are original pilot drafts pending competent safety and native-language review.
